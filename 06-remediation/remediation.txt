REMEDIATION

Vulnerable service:
vsFTPd 2.3.4 on TCP/21

Original xinetd configuration:
disable = no

Backup created:
 /etc/xinetd.d/vsftpd.bak

Remediation:
Changed the xinetd configuration to:

disable = yes

xinetd was restarted using:

/etc/init.d/xinetd restart

Result:
TCP/21 stopped listening.

Verification:
Nmap confirmed:

21/tcp closed

The separate ProFTPD service on TCP/2121 was not modified.
